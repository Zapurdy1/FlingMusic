Why Fling?

Fling Music lets multiple users share their music preference and create a shared playlist. No more fighting over the playlist—everyone gets to hear what gets them movin!

How It Works

  1. Create an account or sign in.
  2. Connect a Spotify account
  3. Generate a one-time QR code to join the room.
  4. Watch as the playlist updates with everyone's tastes!
